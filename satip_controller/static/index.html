    Select up to 16 genuinely free-to-air TV bouquets. Overlapping service references are deduplicated.         Time shift and USB storage

        Live TV does not continuously write to USB. Pause starts time shifting; Return to live stops USB capture and discards its history. A paused position can expire at the storage limit. Use Safely remove USB before unplugging.

        Configuration backup

        Save all receiver settings, channels, favourites and access credentials. Backups are not encrypted: keep them private. TV briefly pauses while a backup is created. Restore replaces settings and reboots; your network address and login may change.
